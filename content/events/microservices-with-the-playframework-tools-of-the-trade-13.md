Microservices with the PlayFramework
-----------------------------------------------------
Dominik Dorn will show us how to create Microservices with the PlayFramework. How to setup a minimal app, how to create restful endpoints, Swagger Integration and publishing using Docker.

Tools of the Trade (if no other talk shows up)
-------------------------
Dominik will share with us some of his experience. Which tools he prefers to work with, which libraries are hot right now, what you should checkout, stuff to follow, concepts to grasp.
