**7 Ways You Can Build an Amazing Dev Career in the AI Era**
Are you also wondering where you fit in all of this?

Every day, you are bombarded with claims that AI will transform software development.

Is this an unprecedented opportunity or an existential threat?
After more than 30 years in the software industry, I've seen technologies create opportunities, destroy opportunities, and completely reshape careers.
Yet... there are always professionals who seem to thrive regardless of the changes happening around them.

Why do some become more valuable... while others struggle?

This presentation will face the myths around AI head-on and explore how you can create opportunities in the age of AI... instead of being afraid and scrambling to keep up.

Because the future may belong to those who understand something deeper than technology itself...

**About Bruno Souza**
Since 1995, Bruno has been helping Java developers advance their careers and work on exciting projects. A recognized Java Evangelist and **Java Champion**, as well as a board member of the **Java Community Process**, Bruno founded and leads SouJava, the Brazilian Java Users Society. In his book, "Developer Career Masterplan," Bruno shares insights on career development for senior developers, topics he further explores in his Code4.life project.
