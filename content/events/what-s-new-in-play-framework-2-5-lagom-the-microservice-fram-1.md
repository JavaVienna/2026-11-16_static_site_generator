Play Framework 2.5 got released and a lot changed. Come to this session to learn whats new, whats special and whats very very useful for your day to day work or special use case!

Also "Lagom" (swedish "just the right amount") got released by Lightbend (formerly Typesafe). Lagom is a microservice framework, designed to easily allow you to create reactive microservice based applications.
