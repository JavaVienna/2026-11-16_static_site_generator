MapDB & Career as an Opensource Project Maintainer
\-\-\-
MapDB:
MapDB provides Java collections backed by a storage engine. It scales up traditional Java API out of the limits imposed by the JVM garbage collector by storing data off-heap or in local files. The project is already over 20 years old, and over time was used by a number of projects such as Ethereum. MapDB has over 4500 Stars on Github.

About Jan Kotek:
Jan Kotek is a software developer from prague and the author of MapDB. Over the years, he worked on number of open source projects, including compilers, IDE plugins, blockchain and database engines. You can find his projects on Github at https://github.com/jankotek or follow him on twitter at https://twitter.com/JanKotek

Kafka&Opensearch @ Willhaben
At Willhaben we rebuilt our "Meine Anzeigen" domain from scratch to match our domain-design we want to implement, and further more upgrade to a more future-proof technology-stack and architecture. In this talk, we present how we leveraged our already existing kafka infrastructure to build an index in AWS opensearch, the challenges we faced during development and how we tackled them.

Matthias has studied computer science at TU Wien and works as a software developer for 8 Years. He joined Willhaben in 2019 and worked there in various projects, with a big focus on restructuring the existing monolithic software into a domain-driven and event-based architecture.
