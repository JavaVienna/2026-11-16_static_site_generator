# Privacy Policy

This is a static website. It does not set tracking cookies and does not collect
personal data from visitors.

## Hosting

The site is served as static HTML. Standard web-server access logs (IP address,
timestamp, requested page) may be recorded by the hosting provider for operational
and security purposes.

## External services

- Event registration happens on [Meetup.com](https://www.meetup.com/java-Vienna/),
  which has its own privacy policy.
- Some speaker and sponsor images may be loaded from external content-delivery
  networks (for example Meetup or LinkedIn) referenced by their original URLs.
